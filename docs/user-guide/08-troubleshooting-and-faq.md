# Troubleshooting and FAQ

This chapter lists the messages you may see, what they mean and what to do. Messages are shown here as they appear in English. Words in quotation marks inside a message, such as ":lesson", are replaced by real names on screen.

## Signing in and registering

| Message or problem | What it means | What to do |
|--------------------|---------------|------------|
| "These credentials do not match our records." | The email or password is wrong. | Check both and try again, or use **Forgot your password?** |
| "Too many login attempts. Please try again in :seconds seconds." | Five failed attempts in a row. | Wait for the time shown, then try again. |
| "This account has been deactivated. Contact an administrator." | Your account has been switched off. | Use the contact form from another device, or contact your organization. |
| "Sign in or create an account to continue (for example, to enroll in a course)." | You tried an action that needs an account. | Log in or register. You are taken back to where you were. |
| "The access code is missing or incorrect for the selected organization." | The Partner access code does not match the organization you chose. | Check that you chose the right organization and ask it for the code. |
| No **Partner** choice on the registration page. | No partner organization is active at the moment. | Register as an Agent, or contact the Academy. |
| "This password reset link is invalid or has expired." | The reset link is older than 60 minutes or was already used. | Choose **Request New Reset Link**. |
| The reset email does not arrive. | It may be in spam, or the address may not be registered (the platform shows the same message either way). | Check spam, request a new link, or contact the Academy. |

## Enrolling

| Message or problem | What it means | What to do |
|--------------------|---------------|------------|
| "Enrollment not available" with "This course is for :for. Log in with an eligible account to enroll." | You are not logged in and the course is restricted to Agents or Partners. | Log in with a suitable account, or choose **Register** (Partner-only courses open the Partner sign-up). |
| "This course is for :for. Your account (:account) can't enroll in it." | Your learner type or organization is not in the audience of this course. | Choose a course made for your category. Learner type cannot be changed on the profile page, so contact the Academy if your type is wrong. |
| "This course is not available for your learner category." (error page, 403) | You tried to enroll in a restricted course without using the page button. | Return to the course catalogue and pick another course. |
| "Please enroll in this course first." | You opened a lesson link of a course that shows a price without being enrolled. | Open the course page and choose **Enroll in this course**. |
| "None of the courses in this bundle are available for your learner category." | Every course in the bundle is restricted to another group. | Choose another bundle or individual courses. |
| A course or bundle page shows "Not found" (404). | The course is not published. | Go back to the catalogue. |

## Lessons

| Message or problem | What it means | What to do |
|--------------------|---------------|------------|
| "This lesson is locked. Finish ":lesson" first to unlock it." | You tried to open a lesson before completing the one before it. | Open the lesson named in the message and finish it. |
| "Take the quiz ":quiz" first to unlock the next lesson." | The lesson before has a quiz that you have not submitted. | Take the quiz (you do not have to pass). |
| **Next lesson** is grey. "Complete this lesson to unlock the next one." | The current lesson has not been recorded as complete. | Read to the end of the page (and stay at least 5 seconds), or watch the video to the end (90% for uploaded videos). |
| "Complete this lesson and take its quiz to unlock the next one." | This lesson has a quiz beneath it. | Complete the lesson, then use **Take the quiz to continue**. |
| "This lesson is locked until the previous lesson is completed." | A request to complete a locked lesson was refused. | Finish the earlier lessons first. |
| "That lesson does not belong to this course." | The lesson link belongs to another course. | Open the lesson from the course page. |
| "Could not save your progress. Please reload the page." | The connection dropped while recording your progress. | Check your internet connection and reload. |
| YouTube lesson will not complete. | Completion is recorded only when the video finishes after you pressed play on the lesson page. | Replay the video on the lesson page and let it finish. |
| "Lesson content will be added soon." | The content is not ready yet. | Come back later. |
| Lessons are not locked in one course but are in another. | A course can allow free order. | No action needed. |

## Quizzes

| Message or problem | What it means | What to do |
|--------------------|---------------|------------|
| "Enroll in the course to take this quiz." / "Enroll in the course first." | You are not enrolled. | Enroll from the course page. |
| "This quiz is not available." | The quiz is not published. | Contact the Academy if you believe it should be available. |
| "This quiz is locked. Finish ":lesson" first to unlock it." | Lessons before the quiz are not complete. | Complete the lesson named. |
| "You have used all :max attempts for this quiz." | No attempts left. | Use **View your last result**. Contact the Academy if you need a further attempt. |
| "You already passed this quiz and cannot retake it." | This quiz does not allow a retake after passing. | Use **View your last result**. |
| "Time is up. Your attempt was closed." | The time limit ended. | Check the result. If attempts remain you can use **Retake quiz**. |
| "The time limit ran out, so the answers you had saved were submitted automatically." | Your saved answers were graded when time ended. | Nothing to do. Answers that were not saved are scored zero. |
| You closed the page in the middle of a quiz. | The attempt is still open. | Open the quiz and choose **Resume quiz**. Any time limit continues to count down. |
| "You already submitted this attempt." | You reopened a finished attempt. | You are shown the result. |

## General questions

**Can I take courses without registering?** You can browse and read course descriptions and curricula, but you must register and log in to enroll and study.

**I registered as the wrong type. Can I change it?** Not yourself. The profile page does not offer this. Use the contact form and explain your situation.

**Do I need to confirm my email?** Not in the current setup (see chapter 2).

**Can I see the correct answers after a quiz?** No. The result shows whether each of your answers was correct, not the right answer.

**Will I get a certificate?** The platform does not issue certificates.

**Can I pay for a course?** The platform has no payment step. Prices shown on some courses are informational, and enrolling is done with the **Enroll in this course** button.

**Is there a search box?** No. Use the category filter on the **Courses** page.

**Can I use the platform on my phone?** Yes. The layout adapts to small screens and the menu opens from a menu button.

**Where is my progress saved?** On your account. Log in from any device to continue.

**Nobody answered my contact message.** The Academy replies to the email address you entered. Check your spam folder, and make sure the address was typed correctly.
