# Live Sessions and Contact

## Live sessions

Some courses include live sessions: scheduled online meetings held outside the platform, for example on a video-call service. The platform lists them and gives you the link. It does not host the meeting itself.

### Where to find them

Live sessions appear on the course page, under **Upcoming live sessions**. Only sessions that have not finished yet are shown. Each entry shows:

* The session title.
* The date, time and length ("Friday, Nov 27, 2026 at 3:00 PM · 60 min").
* A short description, if there is one.

The section is hidden when the course has no upcoming sessions.

### Joining a session

What you see on the right of each session depends on your situation:

| Your situation | What is shown |
|----------------|---------------|
| You were invited | A **Join session** button that opens the meeting link in a new tab, and a **Password** if the meeting has one. |
| You are enrolled but were not invited | "You were not invited to this session." |
| You are not enrolled | "Enroll to be eligible for live sessions." |

If you are invited, you also receive an email titled "Live session invitation" with the course, the date and time, the duration, a **Join the session** button and the meeting password if there is one. The email says you can also open the course page and click **Join session** when the time comes.

> Tip: Check the time zone of your device against the time shown, and join a few minutes early.

The platform does not send reminders and does not record attendance.

## Contact us

Choose **Contact** in the main menu (or **Contact us** in the footer or on the home page).

### Sending a message

1. Fill in **Name** (required).
2. Fill in **Email** (required).
3. Optionally add a **Subject**.
4. Write your question in **Message** (required, up to 5,000 characters).
5. Choose **Send message**.

You will see: "Thanks! We've received your message and will get back to you soon." The administrators read your message and reply to the email address you provided.

The page also lists other ways to reach the Academy (an email address, a phone number and the city, Kigali, Rwanda) under **Other ways to reach us**.

> Tip: If you are asking about a problem with your account, include the email address you registered with.

## About page

Choose **About** in the main menu. The page describes KoraLink Agents Academy, its purpose (becoming a Digital Community Champion), and the kinds of skills taught (**Marketplace skills**, **Customer service** and **Business growth**). It has buttons to explore courses and to contact the Academy. It contains no interactive features.
