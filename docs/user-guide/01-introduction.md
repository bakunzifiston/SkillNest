# Introduction

## What KoraLink Academy is

KoraLink Academy (shown on the site as "KoraLink Agents Academy") is the online learning platform for KoraLink Agents, run under the Digital Jobs for Youth in Health program. It offers practical, self-paced courses in areas such as marketplace skills, customer service and business growth.

You can learn at your own pace. The platform remembers which lessons you have finished, shows your progress, and lets you take quizzes to check what you have learned.

## Who uses the platform

There are three kinds of people on the platform.

| Who | What they can do |
|-----|------------------|
| Visitors (not signed in) | Browse the home page, the course catalogue, course pages, bundles, the About page and the Contact page. Visitors cannot enroll or study. |
| Learners | Everything a visitor can do, plus enroll in courses and bundles, study lessons, take quizzes and manage their profile. |
| Administrators | Manage the platform itself. Administrators are covered in a separate guide and are not described here. |

## Learner types

When you register you choose one of two learner types. The type decides which courses you may enroll in.

| Learner type | Who it is for | Notes |
|--------------|---------------|-------|
| Agent | Independent learners (the default). | You may add optional identity details (see "Registering" in chapter 2). |
| Partner | Learners who join through a partner organization. | You choose your organization and enter its access code. |

Every course has an access level that says who may enroll:

* All learners (everyone, including visitors who register).
* Agents only.
* All Partners.
* One specific partner organization only.

Chapter 3 explains how this looks on screen and what happens if a course is not meant for your account.

## Languages

The platform is available in English and Kinyarwanda. The language switcher shows two buttons, **EN** and **RW**. It is in the top bar of every page and, on a phone, inside the menu under "Language". Chapter 2 explains how your choice is remembered.
