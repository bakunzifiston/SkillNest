# Profile and Account

Open your profile by choosing **Profile** in the top menu (on a phone, open the menu first), or the **Profile** card on your dashboard. You must be logged in.

The page has three sections: **Profile Information**, **Update Password** and **Delete Account**. Under the page title you can see your name and email address.

## Editing your profile information

1. Open **Profile**.
2. Under **Profile Information**, change any of the following:
   * **First name** and **Last name** (both required).
   * **Email** (required, and it must not belong to another account).
   * **Location**: **Country** and, for Rwanda, **Province**, **District** and **Sector**. The same rules apply as when registering (chapter 2).
   * For Agents only: the optional **Additional details**: **Phone number**, **National ID number**, **Date of birth** and **Gender**. The page explains: "Used to verify your identity as an Agent."
3. Choose **Save**. A **Saved.** confirmation appears next to the button.

Partner learners do not have the additional details. If you change your country to something other than Rwanda, your province, district and sector are cleared.

> Note: Your learner type (Agent or Partner) and your partner organization cannot be changed on the profile page. If one of them is wrong, use the contact form (chapter 6).

> Note: Changing your email address marks it as not verified. It does not stop you from using the platform. Use the new address the next time you log in.

## Changing your password

1. Under **Update Password**, enter your **Current Password**.
2. Enter a **New Password** and repeat it in **Confirm Password**. Use at least 8 characters.
3. Choose **Save**. **Saved.** confirms the change.

If the current password is wrong or the two new passwords differ, an error message appears under the relevant field. If you have forgotten your current password, log out and use **Forgot your password?** instead (chapter 2).

## Language preference

There is no language setting on the profile page. You choose your language with the **EN** and **RW** buttons in the top bar. While you are logged in, the choice is saved on your account (chapter 2).

## Deleting your account

> Note: Deleting your account cannot be undone.

1. Under **Delete Account**, choose **Delete Account**.
2. A window asks "Are you sure you want to delete your account?" and explains that all of its resources and data will be permanently deleted.
3. Enter your **Password** to confirm.
4. Choose **Delete Account** in the window, or **Cancel** to keep your account.

If the password is wrong, the window stays open with an error. When the deletion succeeds you are logged out and returned to the home page.

## Logging out

Choose **Log out** in the top menu.
